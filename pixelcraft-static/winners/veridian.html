[03] // AFTER THE HACKATHON

FROM PROTOTYPE
TO CLINICAL PILOT.

SecureScribe has become a focused behavioral-health documentation product. It classifies and routes events locally, flags missing required information instead of inventing it, and keeps a nurse review in the loop before EHR handoff.

  • Runs a proprietary 46-million-parameter classifier on-device rather than sending patient data to a cloud AI service.
  • Now offering a two-month, single-facility paid pilot with defined targets for charting time, completeness, and adoption.
  • Clearly labels those figures as pilot targets—not results—and says its first facility pilot has not yet completed.
